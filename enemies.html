Enemies

asteroids and bosses

Every asteroid has a health bar. Touching one hurts your ship. Waves get faster and tougher as you go, and walls make asteroids bounce.

Asteroids

NORMAL ASTEROID

Small, medium, large. They split.

Tip: Kill the small ones first if you are surrounded.

FAST DART (WAVE 12+)

Arrow shaped. Fast but fragile.

Tip: Fast Laser and Homing make them easy. They run straight at you, so keep distance.

ARMORED (WAVE 22+)

Double outline. Slow, very tough.

Tip: Use Damage, Pierce and Lightning. Do not waste fire on small ones while it chases you.

SPLITTER (WAVE 18+)

Cross inside. Breaks into 3 pieces.

Tip: Explosive cleans up the pieces. Kill it when it is far from you.

Bosses

A boss arrives every 30 waves. Each one has a different attack pattern, and they get angry below half health. Beating a boss gives you better card offers. The image on the left is the boss, and the diagram shows how its bullets move toward your ship.

BOSS 1

THE RING

Rings of bullets. Always the first boss (wave 30).

Tip: Keep moving along the edges and slip through the gaps in the ring. It calls fast asteroids now and then. Slow Time helps a lot.

BOSS 2

THE SNIPER

Fires aimed fans of bullets at you and an occasional ring.

Tip: Do not run in a straight line: change direction after each burst. Stay at a distance and keep shooting.

BOSS 3

THE SPIRAL

Sprays two arms of bullets that spin, then pauses.

Tip: Orbit slowly in the same direction as the spiral. The pauses are your chance to deal damage.

BOSS 4

THE CHARGER

Flashes to warn you, then dashes at your ship, and fires a ring when it stops.

Tip: Move sideways when it flashes. After the dash it is open: hit it now.

BOSS 5

THE SUMMONER

Calls many small asteroids and fires slow rings.

Tip: Kill the small ones quickly with Pierce or Explosive so the screen does not fill up.
