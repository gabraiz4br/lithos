Achievements

21 trophies to unlock

See them in the main menu or in the pause menu. Locked ones show a progress bar, and an "almost there" alert appears when you are close. Runs in God Mode do not count.

Unlock the other 20 to earn the platinum trophy.

Waves

Survivor

Reach wave 10.

Tip: Pick Health, Shield and Damage early.

Veteran

Reach wave 20.

Tip: Multishot and Pierce make a big difference around wave 15.

Living Legend

Reach wave 50.

Tip: You need a strong build. Slow Time and Explosive keep big waves under control.

Immortal

Reach wave 100.

Tip: A very long run. Stack Regen, Shield and Damage. Bosses show up at waves 30, 60 and 90.

Bosses and cards

Boss Hunter

Defeat a boss.

Tip: The first boss is at wave 30. Keep moving along the edges.

Collector

Have 10 different cards in one run.

Tip: 10 different cards in one run, not 10 levels of one card.

Specialist

Take one card to level 10.

Tip: Take the same card 10 times in one run. Common cards are the easiest.

Jackpot

Get a legendary card.

Tip: Legendary cards show up more after boss waves and with Luck.

Laser Rain

Fire 7 lasers at once.

Tip: 6 Multishot cards give 7 lasers per shot.

Swarm

Have 10 drones.

Tip: Swarm gives +3 drones. The Starting Drone upgrade counts too.

Skill

Untouchable

Win a wave 5+ without taking damage.

Tip: Even a hit blocked by a shield counts as a hit.

Close Call

Win a wave 5+ with 1 health.

Tip: Finish the wave with 1 health or less. Risky, so try it with Fury.

Shield Bearer

Block 10 hits with the shield.

Tip: Counts across all runs. Shield cards help.

Totals and score

First Blood

Destroy your first asteroid.

Tip: Shoot any asteroid.

Destroyer

Destroy 1000 asteroids in total.

Tip: Counts across all your runs. God Mode does not count.

Addicted

Play 10 runs.

Tip: Counts every run you finish by dying.

High Scorer

Score 10000 points in one run.

Tip: In a single run. Beating bosses gives a lot of points.

Millionaire

Score 100000 points in one run.

Tip: In a single run. You need a long run with a strong build.

Extras

Engineer

Buy an upgrade in the laboratory.

Tip: Buy any upgrade in the Laboratory.

Hacker

Find the secret code.

Tip: Hint: the code is in the title, and Z is a good key to press on the main menu.

Platinum

🏆 Platinum

Unlock all other achievements.

Tip: Unlock all the other 20 achievements.

Want permanent upgrades? See the Laboratory page.
