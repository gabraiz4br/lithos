The Veins

the card unlock tree

Since v0.6, cards are unlocked in The Veins, a tree you open from the main menu. It has all 23 cards, grouped in 5 veins. Each node costs fragments, the same ones you earn every run.

How it works

  • Common cards are always available (free).
  • Rare cost 40, epic 120 and legendary 300 fragments.
  • You must unlock the previous node of the vein first.
  • At the end of each vein, pick one of two evolutions for 400 fragments. They are small permanent bonuses. You need the last card of the vein first.
  • Press R twice in the tree to refund everything you spent there, and try another plan.
  • Each save has its own tree. Saves from before v0.6 start with every card already unlocked.

Controls

Drag the map with the mouse. Hover a node to read it and click to unlock. With the keyboard, use the arrows and Enter. Q goes back.

The 5 veins

Cannon Vein

Damage → Fire Rate → Fast Laser → Pierce → Multishot → Fury

Evolution (pick one):

  • FAN: +1 LASER PER SHOT.
  • BEAM: +2 PIERCE AND +15% DAMAGE.

Hull Vein

Health → Repair → Shield → Regen → Vampire → Pulse

Evolution (pick one):

  • REFLEX: +1 FREE PULSE LEVEL.
  • BARRIER: +1 SHIELD EVERY WAVE.

Crystal Vein

Ricochet → Homing → Lightning → Storm

Evolution (pick one):

  • CHAIN: LIGHTNING JUMPS +2 TIMES.
  • THUNDER: STORM +2 FREE LEVELS.

Orbit Vein

Thruster → Drone → Slow Time → Swarm

Evolution (pick one):

  • SATELLITE: +1 DRONE.
  • THRUST: +15% SPEED, ASTEROIDS 8% SLOWER.

Core Vein

Luck → Explosive → Supernova

Evolution (pick one):

  • STAR: SUPERNOVA CHARGES 15% FASTER.
  • FORTUNE: +3 LUCK.

The evolutions are small bonuses to the numbers of the game, and their balance is still being tested.
