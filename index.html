About

L.I.T.H.O.S. is a free indie project made by one person. It is still being worked on, so things may change, break or get rebalanced between versions.

Description

You are a lonely triangle against an endless storm of asteroids. Shoot, dodge and use the arena walls to your advantage. After every wave you pick 1 of 3 upgrade cards, and every card has infinite levels. Every 30 waves a boss arrives, each with its own attack pattern.

Earn fragments every run and spend them in the Laboratory on permanent upgrades. 23 cards, 4 rarities, 21 achievements (plus a platinum trophy) and a secret or two. Available in English, Portuguese, Spanish and French. Since v0.6: three save slots, and The Veins, a tree where you unlock cards and pick evolutions.
