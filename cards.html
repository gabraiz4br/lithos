Cards

23 upgrade cards in 4 rarities

After each wave you choose 1 of 3 cards. Every card has infinite levels, so you can pick the same one again. Rarer cards are stronger. The border color shows the rarity.

Rarity colors: Common (gray) · Rare (blue) · Epic (purple) · Legendary (yellow). A boss wave gives better offers, and the Luck card (and the Luck upgrade in the Laboratory) makes rare cards show up more often.

Good combinations

  • Machine gun: Fire Rate + Multishot + Pierce
  • Chain reaction: Explosive + Lightning + Storm
  • Drone army: Drone + Swarm + Damage
  • Glass cannon: Fury + Pulse + Shield
  • Tank: Health + Regen + Vampire + Shield
  • Never miss: Homing + Multishot + Fast Laser

All cards

Damage

Common

+12% Laser damage.

Tip: Scales everything that uses your damage: Explosive, Storm, Drones and Lightning. A safe pick at any time.

Fire Rate

Common

Shoot faster.

Tip: More shots per second. Multiplies the value of Multishot, Pierce and Homing.

Thruster

Common

Faster ship.

Tip: A faster ship dodges more and reaches corners. Great on boss waves with lots of bullets.

Fast Laser

Common

Faster lasers.

Tip: Lasers reach asteroids before they move away. Helps a lot against fast darts.

Health

Common

+3 Max health and heal 3.

Tip: +3 max health and heals 3 right away. Pairs with Regen and Vampire.

Repair

Common

Restore 40% health.

Tip: Only offered when you are hurt. Restores 40% of your health.

Shield

Common

+1 Shield per wave. Blocks a hit.

Tip: Gives a shield every wave that blocks one full hit, even a boss bullet. Stacks each time you pick it.

Ricochet

Common

Lasers bounce +1 time off walls.

Tip: Lasers bounce off the walls. Good when you fight near corners, and with Multishot.

Multishot

Rare

+1 Laser per shot, in a fan.

Tip: Adds a laser to every shot, in a fan. 7 lasers at once unlocks the Laser Rain achievement.

Pierce

Rare

Lasers pierce +1 asteroid.

Tip: Lasers go through asteroids. Excellent with Multishot and Fire Rate against big groups.

Regen

Rare

+1 Health at the end of each wave.

Tip: Heals 1 health at the end of every wave. Good for long runs.

Pulse

Rare

When hit, a wave hurts and pushes.

Tip: When you are hit, a pulse hurts and pushes nearby asteroids. Works well with Health and Fury.

Vampire

Rare

Heal every n kills. N drops per level.

Tip: Heal 1 every few kills. The kills needed go down with each level.

Luck

Rare

Rarer cards appear more often.

Tip: Makes rare, epic and legendary cards show up more often. Pick it early.

Explosive

Epic

Explode on death. More asteroids, more power.

Tip: Asteroids explode when they die. The more asteroids on screen, the stronger it is. Strong with Splitters and Multishot.

Homing

Epic

Lasers chase asteroids.

Tip: Lasers curve toward asteroids. Makes Multishot and Drones almost never miss.

Drone

Epic

+1 Drone that shoots by itself.

Tip: +1 drone that shoots by itself. 10 drones unlock the Swarm achievement.

Lightning

Epic

Lasers jump to nearby asteroids.

Tip: Lasers jump to nearby asteroids for part of the damage. Pairs with Storm.

Slow Time

Epic

Asteroids move slower.

Tip: Asteroids move slower (up to -60%). Makes bosses and dense waves much easier.

Supernova

Legendary

Giant explosion every so often.

Tip: A giant explosion every few seconds. The wait gets shorter with each level.

Storm

Legendary

Lightning strikes random asteroids.

Tip: Lightning strikes random asteroids over and over. More strikes with each level. Pairs with Lightning and Damage.

Swarm

Legendary

+3 Drones that shoot faster.

Tip: +3 drones that also shoot faster. The strongest drone card.

Fury

Legendary

Less health, more damage.

Tip: The less health you have, the more damage you deal. Risky, but great with Pulse, Shield and Regen.
