Laboratory

permanent upgrades

The Laboratory is in the main menu. Every time you die you earn fragments, and you spend them here on upgrades that stay forever, in every future run.

How to get fragments

Fragments = waves × 3 + kills × 0.2 + bosses × 25
Example: reaching wave 20 with 150 kills and no boss = 60 + 30 = 90 fragments. Beating one boss adds 25 more.
Each level of Fragment Hunter adds 10% on top. God Mode gives no fragments.

What to buy first

  1. Fragment Hunter: it pays itself back, because every later run gives more.
  2. Health Base and Damage Base: cheap and good in every run.
  3. Starting Shield and Auto Repair: for surviving longer.
  4. Starting Drone, Piercing Laser, Bouncing Laser and Strong Start: for stronger builds.

Upgrade names on this page may differ slightly from the in-game ones; the effects are the same.

Reset

Press R in the Laboratory, then R again to confirm. All your upgrades go back to level 0 and you get every fragment back, so you can try another plan at no cost.
